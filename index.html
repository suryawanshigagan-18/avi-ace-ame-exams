Help and guidance

Frequently Asked Questions.

Answers to common questions about AVI, aviation examination pathways and learning resources.

What is AVI?

AVI by Bluesky is a platform for global aircraft maintenance engineering exam preparation, bringing together syllabus-aligned study guides, exam-oriented MCQs and realistic mock tests.

Which examination authorities are featured?

The landing page features EASA, DGCA India, FAA, UK CAA, CAAP Philippines, TCCA Canada, CASA Australia, GCAA UAE, GACA Saudi Arabia and CAAS Singapore. Learning availability is shown separately from directory visibility.

What learning resources does AVI bring together?

AVI combines syllabus-aligned Study Guides, examination-oriented MCQ practice and realistic mock assessments. Availability depends on the authority and learning programme.

Is AVI affiliated with EASA or another authority?

No. AVI is an independent examination-preparation platform and is not a regulatory authority or an official examination provider. Always confirm licensing requirements with the relevant competent authority.
